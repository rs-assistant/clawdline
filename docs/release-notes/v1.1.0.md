# Clawdline v1.1.0

**Work moves more smoothly between your Sessions, devices, and machines.**

- **Delegate across paired machines.** An Agent can send a task from a Mac to a Linux machine or back, follow its messages, and receive its result in the original Session. The task keeps its identity through delivery retries and restarts.
- **Find Clawdfather's actions in one place.** Its menu now lives in the Snippet and Workflow sheet, with its own tab for actions you can take. A Session can also create a Workflow where it starts one; only one Workflow runs in that Session at a time.
- **See the plans clearly.** The Plan page shows Free and Pro as separate cards with their current limits. Both plans allow five viewer devices.
- **Catch schedule problems when saving.** A schedule checks its title before it is saved, instead of waiting until a run fails. A one-time schedule keeps the date you chose. Its detail sheet gives the title more room and places Run now directly below it.
- **Manage settings together.** The Settings page brings notification switches, Mac notch and mascot controls, voice, and dispatch rules into one place.
- **See what needs attention.** A checkbox selected on a phone appears immediately on the waiting card. Session lists say why a Session is waiting, and capacity rows have a button that explains their limits. Reading a question again on a phone shows the latest question.
- **Recover from a brief Cloud connection stall.** Machine and Session lists resume updating after a short roster interruption, including the details and available actions for a waiting Session.
- **Remove worktrees without waiting on a frozen dialog.** Removal responds faster, shows that it is working, and lets you close the dialog while it continues.
- **Safer terminal and installation behavior.** Cleaning up a Clawdline private tmux socket no longer stops the main tmux server, and a dev install fails promptly when it cannot acquire the build slot instead of hanging.
- **A smoother first run.** A newly installed machine can reach its terminal from a phone without getting stuck during setup. Copying a numbered transcript list keeps its visible numbers.
